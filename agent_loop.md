# The Agent Loop

An agent loop is how an AI agent thinks and acts over time. It does not just act once — it keeps going, learning from what happened, and trying to do better.

The loop has five steps: **Observe, Decide, Act, Get Feedback, and Improve.**

---

## The Example: Ayesha's Learning Progress

Ayesha is a student on an online learning platform. The AI agent is monitoring her progress and trying to help her stay on track.

---

## Round 1: First Pass Through the Loop

### 1. Observe
The agent looks at the available data.

- Ayesha has not logged in for **9 days**
- She has completed **1 out of 4 assignments**
- Her deadline is approaching

### 2. Decide
The agent thinks about what to do based on what it observed.

> "Ayesha is falling behind. She has missed many days and most of her assignments are incomplete. A reminder email is a gentle, low-effort nudge that might bring her back."

### 3. Act
The agent takes action.

> The agent sends Ayesha a reminder email:
> *"Hi Ayesha, we noticed you haven't logged in for a while. You still have 3 assignments left. You've got this — come back and keep going!"*

### 4. Get Feedback
The agent checks what happened after the action.

- Ayesha did **not open** the email
- She still has **not logged in**
- No assignments were completed

### 5. Improve
The agent reflects on the outcome and updates its thinking.

> "The email did not work. Ayesha may not check her email often, or the message was not urgent enough. I need to try a different channel or a stronger message."

---

## Round 2: The Agent Tries Again

### 1. Observe
Same situation, but now with new data from Round 1.

- Still 9+ days without login
- Email was sent but ignored
- Only 1 of 4 assignments done

### 2. Decide
The agent makes a smarter decision this time.

> "Email did not work. I should try an SMS message or an in-app push notification. I will also involve her instructor, since a personal message from a human may carry more weight."

### 3. Act
The agent takes a stronger action.

> The agent sends an SMS notification AND alerts Ayesha's instructor to reach out personally.

### 4. Get Feedback
The agent waits and watches again.

- Ayesha logs in the next day
- She completes 1 more assignment
- She replies to her instructor

### 5. Improve
The agent updates what it has learned.

> "Direct SMS and instructor involvement worked better than email alone. For students who ignore emails, I should escalate sooner and involve a human contact."

---

## Summary

| Step | What the Agent Does |
|---|---|
| **Observe** | Collects data about the current situation |
| **Decide** | Chooses the best action based on what it sees |
| **Act** | Carries out that action |
| **Get Feedback** | Checks whether the action worked |
| **Improve** | Learns from the result and does better next time |

The agent loop never truly "ends." Each round makes the agent smarter and more effective. That is the core idea behind intelligent, adaptive AI agents.
